# Additional deck artwork research

Checked **2026-09-26**. Five candidate designs were requested. Three could be
prepared as complete playable packs; two still require source artwork.

| Candidate | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Ditha Moser, 1906 | Complete 54 faces and back; nine pip scans have lower native resolution. |
| Adler-Cego | 44 source faces and back; ten pips reconstructed from its own suit marks. |
| Czech Taroky | Historical OTK No. 165: 38 source faces and back, 16 reconstructed pips. Labelled OTK 165, with the actual WWPCM scan catalogue and IPCS pattern history as sources; the current No. 1720 product has different artwork. |
| Chinesen Tarock, Estel 1820 | Only 12 distinct faces located; incomplete. |
| Tarock mit Berufsdarstellungen, Steiger circa 1849 | Product preview only; incomplete. |

See [cards.md](cards.md) for import procedures, source manifests and adaptations.

## Chinesen Tarock

[Piatnik's product page](https://www.piatnik.com/spiele/spielkarten/historische-spielkarten/chinesen-tarock)
identifies article 288494 as a 54-card reproduction of Joseph Estel's Vienna
1820 deck. [The Lower Austrian State Library](https://www.niederoesterreich.gv.at/noe/Landesbibliothek/Highlight_2014.html)
discusses the modern facsimile in September 2014; 1820 is the original's date.

[WOPC](https://www.wopc.co.uk/austria/estel-tarok-1820) supplies ten sample
faces plus the heart ace. [WWPCM's Estel catalogue](http://a.trionfi.eu/WWPCM/decks02/d00498/d00498.htm)
adds only trump VIII to that set. The total is twelve distinct cards.
The 1860 Piatnik Chinese-themed pack and Johann Norbert Hofmann's printing
sheets belong to different decks and cannot fill the missing illustrations.

A complete digitization of Estel's pack or Piatnik's 288494 reproduction is
needed before this can appear in the playable selector.

## Tarock mit Berufsdarstellungen

[Piatnik](https://www.piatnik.com/spiele/spielkarten/historische-spielkarten/tarock-mit-berufsdarstellungen)
identifies article 281198 as a 54-card historical adaptation depicting
occupations and traditional costumes. Its
[official product photograph](https://www.piatnik.com/uploads/media/default/0001/05/e678aceecf221b479a0362481bb4387d49ecf38d.jpeg)
labels the source **Johann Georg Steiger, Wien um 1849**. The photograph
shows three overlapping sample cards and a box, not 54 extractable faces.
[Talon's bulletin 10](https://www.talon.cc/Rundbriefe/Rundbrief%20Nr.%2010.pdf)
records the modern reprint in Piatnik's 2019 catalogue.

The publisher, WWPCM's maker pages and deck index, WOPC, WCMPC, Wikimedia
Commons, Spielkarten-Wiki, museum records and collectors' galleries did not
yield a complete matching set. Other Steiger decks and German occupational
tarocks have different illustrations and were excluded.

A complete 54-card scan set of this reproduction or its corresponding
historical original is needed. No incomplete or invented pack is offered.
